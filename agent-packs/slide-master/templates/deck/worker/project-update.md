# Slide 1 — Cover
**Title:** {project_name} — Update {periode}
**Visual:** Project name + reporting period + presenter
**Speaker note:** ~30 detik.

# Slide 2 — Status Snapshot (RAG)
**Title:** Status Project: {RAG_color}
**Visual:** RAG indicator (Red/Amber/Green) + 3 sub-dimension scores (timeline / scope / budget)
**Speaker note:** TL;DR untuk yang skim. Di-front supaya stakeholder cepet dapat picture. ~45 detik.

# Slide 3 — Overall Progress
**Title:** Progress vs Plan
**Visual:** Gantt mini atau timeline chart, current marker
**Speaker note:** Where we are vs where we should be. ~60 detik.

# Slide 4 — Milestones Achieved
**Title:** Milestones Sejak Update Terakhir
**Visual:** Checklist 3-5 milestones
**Speaker note:** Apa yang udah deliver. Be specific tentang outcomes, bukan activities. ~75 detik.

# Slide 5 — Current Work Streams
**Title:** Stream Aktif
**Visual:** 3-4 stream dengan owner + status + ETA
**Speaker note:** Per stream: what's in flight + who owns + when expected. ~90 detik.

# Slide 6 — Blockers & Risks
**Title:** Blockers + Risk Watch
**Visual:** Per blocker / risk: severity, mitigation, ask
**Speaker note:** Focused. Tidak doom-scroll. Lead dengan blocker yang butuh stakeholder action. ~90 detik.

# Slide 7 — Decisions Needed
**Title:** Decision Needed Hari Ini
**Visual:** Per decision: context, options, recommendation
**Speaker note:** Be explicit tentang ask. "Perlu kamu putuskan A vs B sebelum [deadline]." ~75 detik.

# Slide 8 — Resources & Budget
**Title:** Resources & Budget
**Visual:** Budget burndown chart + headcount status
**Speaker note:** ~45 detik.

# Slide 9 — Customer / User Feedback
**Title:** Customer Signal
**Visual:** 2-3 quotes atau metric (NPS, CSAT, usage)
**Speaker note:** Optional. Pakai kalau project punya customer-facing component. ~60 detik.

# Slide 10 — Next Phase Plan
**Title:** Phase Selanjutnya
**Visual:** Timeline dengan 3-5 milestones depan
**Speaker note:** What's coming. Set expectation. ~60 detik.

# Slide 11 — Asks / Support Needed
**Title:** Ask
**Visual:** 2-3 specific asks
**Speaker note:** Recap concrete asks. Ulangi yang sudah disebut di slide 6 + 7 kalau perlu. ~30 detik.

# Slide 12 — Q&A / Diskusi
**Title:** Diskusi
**Visual:** Kosong
**Speaker note:** Buka Q&A. Punya cheat-sheet for FAQ-able topics (timeline detail, budget detail). ~5 menit Q&A.

---

> _Catatan: RAG color logic — Red = blocker tanpa mitigation; Amber = at-risk tapi ada plan; Green = on track. Surface RAG di slide 2 supaya stakeholder yang skim langsung dapat status._
