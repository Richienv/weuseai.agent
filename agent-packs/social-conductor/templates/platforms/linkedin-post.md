# Template — LinkedIn long-form post

> Dipakai `post-drafter` saat platform = `linkedin`. LinkedIn favors substantial posts dengan line break per paragraf. Hook drives the "see more" click. Hard cap 1300 char di sweet spot, total max 1500 sebelum truncate keras.

---

## Variables

| Variable | Wajib | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `{hook_line}` | ya | Line pertama. Drives "see more" click. 80-150 char optimal |
| `{hook_expansion}` | ya | Line 2-3, ngebantu hook tapi belum reveal. Total preview ≤220 char |
| `{paragraph_1}` | ya | 1 kalimat per paragraf. Context atau setup |
| `{paragraph_2}` | ya | Build atau tension |
| `{paragraph_3}` | ya | Reveal atau lesson learned |
| `{paragraph_4}` | tidak | Optional: data point atau third reflection |
| `{closer_line}` | ya | Reflection atau soft CTA. Bukan hard sell |
| `{hashtag_block}` | ya | 3-5 tag, professional + niche. LinkedIn likes fewer tags |

---

## Template

```
{hook_line}

{hook_expansion}

{paragraph_1}

{paragraph_2}

{paragraph_3}

{paragraph_4}

{closer_line}

{hashtag_block}
```

### Contoh terisi (register: calm-reflective, topic: voice consistency saat agency scale)

```
Voice brand bukan masalah taste. Itu masalah operations.

Aku baru selesai audit 4 agency kecil di Jakarta. Pola yang sama muncul di tiga dari empat.

Dua orang nulis caption. Awalnya satu voice.

Bulan ketiga, copywriter junior masuk. Tone bergeser tipis tapi konsisten ke arah baru.

Bulan keenam, audience drop-off — bukan karena content jelek, tapi karena brand-nya berasa beda dari yang mereka follow dulu.

Yang berhasil di-fix: lock voice profile dari 20 sample writing, lalu setiap draft baru di-score fit-nya sebelum kirim.

Voice yang tetap = audience yang tetap. Lebih murah daripada re-acquisition cost.

#brandvoice #socialmediastrategi #agencyops #brandconsistency #contentleadership
```

## Tone guide — LinkedIn

- **Opening style:** observation atau insight, bukan pertanyaan retoris. "Voice brand bukan masalah taste" works. "Pernah ngga sih..." doesn't
- **Hook line char:** 80-150 optimal. Drives the "see more" click. Punchy, claim-driven
- **Paragraph length:** 1-2 kalimat per paragraf. Line break antar paragraf. Wall-of-text = scroll-past
- **Total char:** 600-1300 sweet spot. Algorithm favors substantial posts. Cutoff ~1500 sebelum "see more" hard
- **Register:** calm, reflective, data-aware. Tidak hustle culture, tidak motivational guru. Jakarta professional yang dihormati di sektornya
- **Hashtag:** 3-5 tag professional + niche. Bukan #hustle #grindset. Lowercase, no spam
- **Punctuation:** zero exclamation marks. Em dash boleh untuk emphasis. Bullet list boleh kalau ada 3+ item paralel
- **Personal vs corporate:** LinkedIn tetap lebih personal-led — first person, share pengalaman atau observasi, bukan press release. "Aku audit 4 agency" > "Kami menemukan bahwa"
- **Closer yang work:** reflection, soft CTA, atau open question. Bukan "DM saya kalau tertarik konsultasi"
- **Banned LinkedIn-isms:** "Thoughts?", "What do you think?", "Agree or disagree?" — feel template. Pakai opening yang sesuai topic
- **Emoji:** 0-1 per post. LinkedIn audience lean ke zero-emoji
