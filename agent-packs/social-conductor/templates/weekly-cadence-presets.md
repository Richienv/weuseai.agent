# Weekly Cadence Presets

> Reference yang dipakai `content-calendar-builder` saat customer pilih preset, atau saat suggest cadence untuk customer baru.

---

## Presets

### Solopreneur — 3 posts/week
**Profile:** founder bisnis kecil, founder-content brand, jualan service / produk simpel.
**Cadence:**
- Mon: long-form (LinkedIn long post atau blog)
- Wed: video pendek (TikTok / Reels)
- Fri: engagement post (Q&A, story, conversational)

**Rationale:** sustainable solo, leave bandwidth untuk biz operasi. Weekly rhythm clear.

### Brand — Daily, mixed format
**Profile:** small-medium brand, ada team minimal 2 (founder + content lead).
**Cadence:**
- Mon: behind-scenes / team
- Tue: product / service highlight
- Wed: educational long-form
- Thu: customer story / testimonial
- Fri: thought leadership / opinion
- Sat-Sun: lighter content (memes, micro-quote, repost)

**Rationale:** consistent daily presence, content mix avoid one-note feed.

### Creator — 1-2/day, batch shooting
**Profile:** full-time creator, audience-build phase, monetize via brand deal / own product.
**Cadence:**
- Daily morning (10-11 AM WIB): main content piece
- Daily evening (7-9 PM WIB): supplementary (story, lighter post, engagement-pull)
- Batch shoot Monday: produce week's worth of content in 1 day

**Rationale:** algorithmic feeding, audience expectation set, batch for efficiency.

### Minimal — 1 post/week
**Profile:** brand-build mode, no urgency on growth, premium positioning.
**Cadence:**
- 1 post per week, high-quality, timed for max audience window

**Rationale:** quality over quantity. Premium brands often benefit from scarcity.

### Campaign-burst — variable
**Profile:** standard cadence + bursts during campaign windows.
**Cadence base:** solopreneur or brand preset.
**During campaign:** +50-100% daily output for 2-4 weeks.

**Rationale:** campaign moments demand higher signal volume, then return to base.

| Platform | Weekday primary | Weekday secondary | Weekend |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 7-9 PM | 12-1 PM | 11 AM-2 PM |
| Reels | 7-9 PM | 6-7 AM | 10 AM-12 PM |
| LinkedIn | 8-10 AM | 5-7 PM (M-Th) | (skip) |
| X | 7-8 AM, 12-1 PM, 5-6 PM | continuous engagement | low priority |
| Blog | (less time-sensitive — surface via newsletter / search) |
| Threads | 7-9 PM | 12 PM | 10 AM-12 PM |

**Caveat:** windows are starting points. Customer's audience may differ. Adjust after 4 weeks of data.

## Theme rotation defaults (kalau customer belum decide)

### Solopreneur (4-week rotation)
- W1: Tips / how-to (educational anchor)
- W2: Behind-the-scenes (humanize brand)
- W3: Customer / community spotlight
- W4: Reflection / thought (personal voice)

### Creator (4-week rotation)
- W1: Niche-deep educational
- W2: Trend-ride content (one beat, brand-consistent)
- W3: Personal story / behind-scenes
- W4: Engagement-pull (Q&A, polls, AMA)

### Brand (4-week rotation)
- W1: Product / service
- W2: Education adjacent niche
- W3: Customer voice
- W4: Industry / category POV

## Cadence health metrics

Track via `content-calendar-builder` weekly-review mode:
- **Slot completion rate**: posted / planned
- **Draft lead time**: avg days between draft-ready and posted
- **Skip rate**: skipped / planned (>20% skipped = cadence too aggressive, recalibrate)
- **Voice fit drift**: avg fit score across week (track via voice-consistency-checker)

If skip rate >25% for 2 consecutive weeks, suggest cadence step-down.
