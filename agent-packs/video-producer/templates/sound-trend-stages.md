# Sound Trend Stages Reference

> Framing untuk sound-trend-tracker. Aku surface stage + decision criteria; customer verifikasi sound spesifik di Trending tab platform.

---

## The 4 stages

### 1. Emerging (Day 1-7 of trend)
**Signal:** Sound muncul di 50-500 video baru per minggu, growing fast. Belum hit mainstream.
**Adopt?** **Yes** — high upside, low competition. Algoritma reward early adoption.
**Risk:** Trend bisa fizzle. Hedge dengan brand-fit sound, bukan asal naik.

### 2. Peak (Day 7-21)
**Signal:** Sound di 5k-50k video baru per minggu. Hit Top Sounds di Trending tab.
**Adopt?** **Yes, but fast** — masih ada window 7-10 hari. Audience kenal sound = lower friction.
**Risk:** Saturated quickly. Late entry = blend with crowd, hook butuh kerja extra.

### 3. Decay (Day 21+)
**Signal:** Volume drop 20%+ week-over-week. Top creators sudah pivot.
**Adopt?** **Skip** — algoritma sudah penalize "late trend". Audience signal "stale".
**Exception:** Brand niche-specific can ride decay sound kalau brand-voice match.

### 4. Evergreen
**Signal:** Sound exists 6+ months tanpa peak-decay cycle (mis. native acoustic Indonesia, lo-fi background).
**Adopt?** **Yes, low-risk** — slow burn but always-on. Good for brand-build content.
**Risk:** Less algorithmic boost than emerging trend.

## Decision matrix

| Goal | Emerging | Peak | Decay | Evergreen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reach (algorithmic boost) | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★ | ★★ |
| Engagement (qualified comments) | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★★★ |
| Brand-build (consistency) | ★★ | ★★★ | ★ | ★★★★★ |
| Cost (production effort) | ★★★★ (low) | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★★★ |

## How to verify sound stage (customer-side)

1. **TikTok Creator Center** → Trending tab → check usage growth chart
2. **Reels Trending Reels** tab → Filter by location (Indonesia)
3. **YouTube Shorts** sound search → check "First used" date + recent uploads count
4. **TokBoard / Trends.co** (third-party) — directional, not authoritative

Aku **bukan** scraper; aku surface framing. Customer verifikasi 30 detik di platform sebelum commit.

## Indonesian-context sound categories

Per niche, sound kategori yang historically populer di Indonesia:

### Fintok / keuangan
- Anxious-comedic ("me trying to manage budget")
- Reflective acoustic Indonesia
- Old-school dangdut remix (decay risk: cycles cepat)

### Kuliner / food
- Native acoustic Indonesia (evergreen)
- ASMR cooking ambient (evergreen)
- "What I eat in a day" remixed audio (peak cycles)

### Fashion / lifestyle
- City lo-fi Jakarta (evergreen)
- TikTok-original chill remix (peak)
- 90s-2000s nostalgia Indonesian pop (cycle)

### Tech / IT
- Cinematic-techno (evergreen)
- "Pov: kamu programmer" comedic narration audio (peak)
- AI / startup motivational instrumental (evergreen, niche)

### Edukasi
- Calm piano instrumental (evergreen)
- "Did you know" voice-over format with claps (peak cycles)

---

## Brand-fit override

Sound trend yang **ngga match brand voice** = skip, berapapun stage-nya. Better blend ke evergreen sound brand consistent daripada chase trend sambil betray brand voice.

Filter: *"Would someone I respect in Jakarta save this video, or scroll past?"*
