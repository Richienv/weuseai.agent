## Why this exists

After Phase 4 (capability graduation foundation), the platform has 10 personas, an autobrowse harness, video render, PII-redacting transforms, and kanban scaffolding. **None of these are stitched into a coherent "we built your business with you" experience.**

The founder's vision (per `phase_5_cofounder_ambition.md`): a customer pays Studio tier (Rp 5.9jt setup + Rp 99k/bulan) and gets a **single Master Agent — Business Director v3** that walks them from `idea` → `incorporated company with first 10 paying customers`. The Master Agent dispatches to 10 specialist personas and 5 department skill packs (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Legal, Finance) — most of which already exist as primitives from Phase 2-4. Phase 5 is the **integration + state-machine + approval-gate layer** that makes the cofounder ambition real for Studio customers.

Phase 5 is **NOT** the full Cofounder-Clone (that's Phase 6+):
- No persistent department workspaces with simulated head-count.
- No custom codebase integration for product roadmap auto-tracking.
- No long-running multi-week autonomous execution without explicit founder check-ins.

Phase 5 IS:
- **5-stage roadmap state machine** with explicit deliverable gates per stage.
- **Department skill packs** that bundle existing personas behind a domain-shaped facade.
- **Indonesian-context business operations** baked in: PT/CV setup, OSS-RBA, BPJS, payment gateway integrations, local banks.
- **Approval gates** for irreversible / dangerous actions (incorporate, sign contracts, transfer money).
- **Master agent orchestration** via Hermes v0.13.x native kanban (Project Conductor pattern, already shipped).

## Locked scope

### 5-stage Indonesian-founder roadmap

Already defined in Persona v2 Day 5 (`agent-packs/business-director/templates/roadmap/5-stage-checklist.md`). Phase 5 adds the **state machine** that tracks customer progression + auto-prompts next-stage transitions.

| Stage | Goal | Deliverables (must-have) | Default duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea | Validate market signal | 15-20 customer interviews, competitor scan, value-prop one-liner, niche-1 customer profile | 2-6 weeks |
| 2. Setup | Legal foundation | PT/CV decided, akta notaris, NPWP badan, OSS NIB, rekening badan, BPJS (kalau hire) | 2-4 weeks |
| 3. Identity | Brand + minimum infra | Brand name + tagline, visual identity, landing/multi-page site, payment gateway, WA Business + email automation | 1-2 weeks |
| 4. Build | First 10 paying customers | MVP product, customer support flow, T&C + privacy, basic analytics | 4-8 weeks |
| 5. Sell | Repeatable acquisition + retention engine | Top 2-3 channels identified, content engine, retention triggers, referral program, tax compliance auto-set | ongoing |

**State transitions** are gated by deliverable completion. Customer can't move from `idea` → `setup` until at least the customer interview log + value prop are persisted. BD v3 enforces; founder can override via admin tool.

### Department skill packs

5 packs that bundle existing personas behind a domain facade. Customer doesn't pick which persona; the pack chooses.

| Department | Persona stack | Phase 5 facade skill |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | The Pro (channel strategy) + Social Conductor (channel-specific tactics) | `sales-pack:plan-channel-strategy`, `sales-pack:run-customer-interview`, `sales-pack:draft-cold-outreach` |
| **Marketing** | Web Creator + Social Conductor + Video Producer | `marketing-pack:launch-campaign`, `marketing-pack:weekly-content-calendar`, `marketing-pack:landing-page-pivot` |
| **Engineering** | extend-capabilities (BYOK LLM technical scoping) | `eng-pack:scope-feature`, `eng-pack:write-tech-spec`, `eng-pack:debug-issue` |
| **Legal** | Doc Expert (contract templates + Indonesian compliance docs) | `legal-pack:draft-contract`, `legal-pack:review-tos`, `legal-pack:compliance-check-monthly` |
| **Finance** | Trade Pro (pricing + capital + IDR-BI context) | `finance-pack:pricing-tier-review`, `finance-pack:cash-flow-projection`, `finance-pack:capital-strategy` |

**Pack design rules:**
1. Pack skills are **routing facades** — they don't contain new logic; they translate department-shaped customer intents into the right specialist persona's existing skill schema.
2. Packs respect persona tier eligibility. If a pack tries to call a Pro+ skill but customer is Starter (rare in Studio context), fall back to a degraded variant.
3. Packs are part of the `business-director` agent pack (lives at `agent-packs/business-director/skills/<pack-name>/`).

### Indonesian-context business operations

Five integration surfaces that make BD v3 specifically Indonesian-founder useful (vs generic SMB cofounder tooling):

1. **DJP / OSS-RBA** — incorporation walkthrough using `business-director/skills/incorporation-advisor` (already shipped Persona v2 Day 5). Phase 5 adds: post-OSS-NIB verification scheduler, KBLI risk-tier auto-detection, dokumen pendukung due-date tracker.

2. **BPJS Kesehatan + Ketenagakerjaan** — auto-detect when customer hires first karyawan (signal: "hire", "rekrut", "karyawan baru" in chat) → trigger `compliance-checker` skill with `has_employees: true`.

3. **Payment gateways** — Xendit + Midtrans + DOKU integration recommendations + actual setup walkthroughs. Phase 5 adds: per-gateway side-by-side cost calculator (MDR rates per payment method × expected volume → monthly fee comparison).

4. **Local banks** — BCA / Mandiri / BNI rekening badan opening walkthroughs (different docs required per bank; document checklist + appointment flow).

5. **Tax filing** — `compliance-checker` skill (already shipped) ping H-7 / H-1 / D-day for PPh 21, PPh 25, PPh Final UMKM, PPN, SPT Tahunan Badan, BPJS bulanan. Phase 5 adds: auto-generate filing checklist per due date with Indonesian tax-portal links.

### Master Agent: Business Director v3

Extends the existing Persona v2 BD (`agent-packs/business-director/`) — same SOUL.md voice, same 5-stage roadmap awareness, plus:

- **State persistence** — tracks customer's current stage + per-stage deliverable status in DB.
- **Department dispatching** — picks the right pack per intent.
- **Approval queue** — surfaces dangerous actions to customer before execution.
- **Cross-session memory** — recalls "last week we agreed to X" without customer re-explaining.
- **Kanban orchestration** — for multi-step initiatives (e.g. product launch), creates a kanban board via Project Conductor's `kanban-orchestrator` skill, dispatches department-tagged tasks, monitors progress.

### Approval gates

Customer-confirmation required for irreversible actions. Approval queue lives in DB; surfaced in BD v3's responses + via Telegram nudges if customer is offline.

| Action category | Examples | Approval mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorporate** | Submit OSS NIB, sign akta notaris, register PT at AHU | Customer must reply "approve" or click signed-link in Telegram |
| **Contract sign** | Cofounder agreement, vendor contract, employment agreement | Same as above + lawyer review nudge |
| **Money transfer** | Pay setup fee, transfer to vendor, payroll | Hard refuse — BD v3 surfaces the action + customer initiates manually |
| **Public emission** | Post to social, send mass email, publish landing page to production domain | Approval before fire (preview in BD's chat first) |
| **Regulatory filing** | Submit SPT, file BPJS adjustment, lapor PPN | Surface checklist; customer files at portal pajak / BPJS portal — we never file on customer's behalf |

### Tier locked: Studio only (Rp 5.9jt setup + Rp 99k/bulan)

Phase 5's enabled_for_tiers is `["studio"]` across all department packs and the BD v3 enhancements. Pro and Starter customers continue with the existing Persona v2 BD scoped MVP (5-stage roadmap surface, no department dispatch, no kanban orchestration).

Pricing rationale (locked):
- Studio customers expect cofounder-quality service. Rp 5.9jt setup buys 1-on-1 onboarding + admin attention.
- Rp 99k/bulan covers ongoing infra (VPS + LLM credits via BYOK, auto-suspend after 30 days inactive).
- Phase 5 doesn't change tier pricing.

## Architecture

### Schema migrations needed

Three new tables + 1 column on existing customer table. All applied via Supabase Management API (token live since 2026-05-10).

```sql
-- 20260510100000_phase_5_master_agent_state.sql

-- 1. Per-customer stage tracker.
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS public.business_roadmap_state (
  customer_id     uuid primary key references public.customers(id) on delete cascade,
  current_stage   text not null check (current_stage in ('idea','setup','identity','build','sell')),
  stage_entered_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  -- Per-stage deliverable completion. Keys = locked deliverable IDs from
  -- agent-packs/business-director/templates/roadmap/5-stage-checklist.md.
  deliverables_completed jsonb not null default '{}'::jsonb,
  -- Free-form founder notes (BD v3 reads + writes).
  notes_md        text,
  updated_at      timestamptz not null default now()
);

CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_roadmap_state_stage
  ON public.business_roadmap_state (current_stage);

-- 2. Approval queue.
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS public.approval_requests (
  id              uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  customer_id     uuid not null references public.customers(id) on delete cascade,
  action_kind     text not null check (action_kind in (
    'incorporate', 'contract_sign', 'public_emission', 'regulatory_filing'
  )),
  action_summary  text not null,            -- Human-readable for surfacing
  action_payload  jsonb not null,           -- Full structured action
  proposed_by_agent text not null,          -- e.g. 'business-director' or 'sales-pack'
  status          text not null check (status in (
    'pending', 'approved', 'rejected', 'expired'
  )) default 'pending',
  approved_by     text,                     -- 'customer' / 'founder-admin'
  approved_at     timestamptz,
  expires_at      timestamptz not null,     -- per-action default (Q4=C): incorporate=14d, contract_sign=14d, public_emission=24h, regulatory_filing=48h. Handler computes from action_kind on insert.
  created_at      timestamptz not null default now()
);

CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_approval_pending
  ON public.approval_requests (customer_id, status, expires_at)
  WHERE status = 'pending';

-- 3. Department thread tracker (which department packs are currently engaged).
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS public.department_threads (
  id              uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  customer_id     uuid not null references public.customers(id) on delete cascade,
  department      text not null check (department in (
    'sales', 'marketing', 'engineering', 'legal', 'finance'
  )),
  initiative_summary text not null,         -- "Q3 product launch", "Tax filing prep", etc.
  -- Optional kanban board reference (kanban runs in Hermes; we mirror id only).
  hermes_kanban_board_id text,
  status          text not null check (status in (
    'active', 'paused', 'closed'
  )) default 'active',
  created_at      timestamptz not null default now(),
  closed_at       timestamptz
);

CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_dept_threads_customer
  ON public.department_threads (customer_id, status);

-- 4. customers row gets phase_5_enabled flag (gates BD v3 features per Studio tier).
ALTER TABLE public.customers
  ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS phase_5_enabled boolean not null default false;

-- 5. RLS — service-role only writes; customer can SELECT their own state.
ALTER TABLE public.business_roadmap_state ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
ALTER TABLE public.approval_requests ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
ALTER TABLE public.department_threads ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;

DROP POLICY IF EXISTS "anon read own roadmap state" ON public.business_roadmap_state;
CREATE POLICY "anon read own roadmap state"
  ON public.business_roadmap_state FOR SELECT
  TO anon
  USING (true);

DROP POLICY IF EXISTS "anon read own approvals" ON public.approval_requests;
CREATE POLICY "anon read own approvals"
  ON public.approval_requests FOR SELECT
  TO anon
  USING (true);

DROP POLICY IF EXISTS "anon read own dept threads" ON public.department_threads;
CREATE POLICY "anon read own dept threads"
  ON public.department_threads FOR SELECT
  TO anon
  USING (true);
```

### Edge Functions specified

Three new Edge Functions. All non-blocking on `SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN` (already live).

| Function | Purpose | Auth |
|---|---|---|
| `roadmap-state-handler` | GET/PATCH customer roadmap state. Customer reads their own; BD v3 writes via service-role. | verify-jwt (anon for read; service-role for write via internal flow) |
| `approval-queue-handler` | List pending approvals + accept/reject endpoint. Customer-facing. | verify-jwt |

### State machine: 5-stage progression

```
[Idea]
  ├─ Customer interviews logged ≥15? --no--> stay
  ├─ Value prop one-liner finalized? --no--> stay
  ├─ Niche-1 profile defined? --no--> stay
  ├─ All three? --yes--> propose transition to Setup
  └─ Stuck >2 months? --signal pivot prompt--

[Setup]
  ├─ PT vs CV decided? --no--> route to incorporation-advisor
  ├─ Akta notaris signed? --no--> approval gate (incorporate)
  ├─ NPWP badan + OSS NIB? --no--> walkthrough
  ├─ Rekening badan opened? --no--> bank doc checklist
  ├─ All four? --yes--> propose transition to Identity
  └─ Stuck >2 months? --suggest hire akuntan/notaris jasa--

[Identity]
  ├─ Brand name + tagline? --no--> route to The Pro
  ├─ Landing site live? --no--> route to Web Creator
  ├─ Payment gateway active? --no--> walkthrough Xendit/Midtrans
  ├─ WA Business + email automation? --no--> Social Conductor
  ├─ All four? --yes--> propose transition to Build

[Build]
  ├─ MVP deliverable? --no--> route to engineering pack
  ├─ First 10 customer feedback synthesized? --no--> route to Deep Researcher
  ├─ Customer support flow live? --no--> Social Conductor
  ├─ T&C + privacy + refund policy? --no--> route to legal pack
  ├─ All four? --yes--> propose transition to Sell

[Sell]
  ├─ Top 2-3 channels identified? --no--> route to sales pack
  ├─ Channel content engine running? --no--> marketing pack
  ├─ Retention triggers active? --no--> Social Conductor
  ├─ Tax compliance auto-set? --no--> compliance-checker
  └─ Reached 10-20 customers/month? --yes--> stay (steady-state) or
                                    --no--> revisit retention + acquisition mix
```

State transitions trigger:
- Telegram notification ("Stage transition: Setup → Identity. Aku surface 5 hal next yang biasanya stuck...")
- Approval queue entry for the explicit-transition acknowledgment.
- Audit log entry for founder admin tooling.

## Locked decisions (Q1-Q5)

**Locked 2026-05-09. Picks: A, A, A, C, C.** All implementation downstream of this section assumes these decisions.

### Q1 — Stage 2 naming: **A. Keep "Setup"**
"Setup" is more concrete than "Initial" for Indonesian founders ("setup PT" is a common phrase). Renaming has zero new value and would break the existing roadmap-tracker schema enum. The 5-stage roadmap remains: `idea → setup → identity → build → sell`.

### Q2 — Customer approval mechanism: **A. Telegram-only**
For Phase 5, approvals live in the customer's existing per-customer Telegram pairing chat. `approval-queue-handler` formats the pending request and posts to Telegram; customer replies `approve <id>` or `reject <id>` (and optionally a free-text reason). Dashboard `/approvals` UI is deferred to Phase 5.5+ if customers complain. Telegram is already the canonical channel — keeps surface area minimal.

### Q3 — Department pack tier eligibility: **A. All 5 packs Studio-only**
All 5 department packs (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Legal, Finance) gate on `customers.tier = 'studio' AND customers.phase_5_enabled = true`. Pro+ customers receive the existing Persona v2 BD scoped MVP without department dispatch. Phase 5 IS the Studio differentiator — mixing tiers dilutes the cofounder-ambition framing. Manifest entries for all pack-routing skills carry `enabled_for_tiers: ["studio"]`.

### Q4 — Approval expiry: **C. Per-action-kind defaults**
Different action kinds have different urgency profiles:

| `action_kind` | Default expiry | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `incorporate` | 14 days | Akta notaris signing rarely happens within 7 days |
| `contract_sign` | 14 days | Customer often forwards to legal counsel for review |
| `public_emission` | 24 hours | Social posts / outbound email need fast turnaround |
| `regulatory_filing` | 48 hours | DJP / OSS deadlines have hard cliffs |

`approval-queue-handler` computes `expires_at = now() + interval[action_kind]` on insert. Override via explicit `expires_at` in the request body if a specific case warrants. A nightly cron flips `pending → expired` past the cliff.

### Q5 — BD v3 cross-session memory: **C. Hybrid (DB decisions log + Hermes context)**
Two-layer model:
