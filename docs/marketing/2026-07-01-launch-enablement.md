## 1. What weuseai.agent is

weuseai.agent is an AI agent that runs on your own private server, greets you first with a morning briefing, and does real work — drafting your letters, decks, content calendars, and replies in your writing style, remembering context across days, all through a simple Telegram chat (text or voice note). It is not a chatbot you have to keep prompting. You set it up once, it lives on a dedicated VPS that runs 24/7, and it comes pre-loaded with 190+ Indonesia-specific templates and a team of specialist personas tuned to your work. The core discipline: the agent prepares and drafts; you approve and send. It never acts on your behalf without your sign-off.

**One-line promise:**
> **Satu agent. Ngerjain kerja kamu.** — Dia nyapa kamu duluan, briefing pagi masuk sebelum diminta. Kamu cukup setujui.

**Section thesis (use as a sub-headline):**
> **Chat menjawab. Agent mengerjakan.**

## 10. ⚠️ Honest-Claims Boundary (READ FIRST)

These were caught being over-stated, and some are **currently live** in landing/use-cases copy. Do not amplify them in campaigns. Each entry gives the honest version to say instead.

### 10.1 NEVER: "the agent auto-sends / auto-posts / auto-emails for you"
The agent is **approval-first by design.** It drafts and shows; you click send.
- **Do not repeat (live over-claims):** "auto-kirim WA ke setiap prospek," "Auto-jadwalin posting," "auto-bikin brosur," a hero animation showing "3 invoice terkirim" auto-executed.
- **Honest version:** *"Agent menyiapkan draft dan usulan tindakan. Kamu approve sekali tap, agent yang jalanin."* A standing mode for low-risk repeat tasks is real, but frame it as opt-in and scoped — never as unattended sending of high-stakes messages.

### 10.2 NEVER: live Gmail / Google Calendar / WhatsApp / LinkedIn / CRM integration
Transport is **Telegram only.** There is no WhatsApp gateway, no Google Calendar wiring, no live email/CRM access. Outbound email is transactional from our system, not from the customer's Gmail.
- **Do not repeat (live over-claims):** listing the channel as "Telegram/WhatsApp"; "auto-baca semua kalender"; "Auto-Reply Customer WhatsApp 24/7"; "cek database nasabah kamu"; "auto-cari LinkedIn, IG"; "sejarah email kamu sama mereka."
- **Honest version:** *"Kamu pakai agent lewat Telegram — teks atau voice note."* For email: *"Agent bisa menyiapkan email profesional dari template; kamu yang setujui sebelum terkirim."* Do not name Gmail, Calendar, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or CRM as connected integrations. The WhatsApp button on Enterprise is a sales-contact link only.

### 10.3 NEVER: fabricated scarcity or fake countdown timers
The scarcity counter MUST read the real live count from the subscription-count endpoint. If it can't, show nothing — never fabricate a number. A clock-based price deadline is forbidden.
- **Do not repeat / do not screenshot for ads (live over-claims):** hardcoded "363 / 500" and "137 slot," a fake MM:SS countdown timer, a random-number-driven "247×" reuse counter.
- **Honest version:** use the real live number, or say nothing. Allowed FOMO: *"Harga naik ke Rp 999rb setelah 1.000 pelanggan pertama"* — count-based, no clock.

### 10.4 NEVER: unverifiable precision stats ("99% akurat", "247× reuse")
No accuracy measurement exists. These are decorative UI values, not measured metrics.
- **Do not repeat (live over-claims):** "Akurat 99% seterusnya," a "Dikerjakan agent 99%" bar, the animated "247×" figure.
- **Honest version:** describe the mechanism, not a fake percentage — *"Ajarin sekali, agent ingat cara kamu dan mengulanginya."* Any stat you cite must be one the team actually measured (the setup-time line *"Sekitar 8 menit — angka yang kami ukur, bukan janji"* is the correct model).

### 10.5 NEVER: invented competitor prices or comparisons
No competitor pricing data exists in the product. Any "vs [competitor] Rp X" would be fabricated.
- **Honest version:** state only our own verified prices — setup from Rp 399rb (Solo) / Rp 799rb (Library Lengkap, anchor ~~Rp 999rb~~) / Rp 1.299rb (Siap Pakai); Rp 99rb/bulan operational; Always-On +Rp 49rb/bulan. The ChatGPT/Claude Pro "$20–30/bulan" reference is public list pricing and fine to cite; do not invent numbers for anyone else.

### 10.6 Consistency guard — keep numbers aligned
- **Setup time:** use **~8 menit**, framed as measured. Do not say "5 menit."
- **Personas:** **10** canonical customer-facing personas (Library Lengkap = all 10). Do not say "8" for the full library (8 is the Siap Pakai set).
- **Templates:** **"190+ template"** is safe (300+ actually ship). Do not inflate further.
- **Display names:** use "Web Creator" and "Business Director" — never the internal slugs.
