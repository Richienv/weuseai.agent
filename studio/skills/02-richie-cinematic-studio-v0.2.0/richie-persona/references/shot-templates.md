# Shot template — "Now-You vs. Younger-You" (Messi "YOU / NEXT LEVEL" style)

A proven 5-shot motivational sequence. Five 16:9 film stills, all on the premium standard in
`cinematic-shots.md` (same camera/grade, only the lens changes). Story: the **current self** (built
things abroad, ZJU hoodie) faces the **younger self** (wanted to stay and build Indonesia, red
Indonesia jersey).

1. **WIDE** — current self alone on a dark misty floodlit field, exhausted, off-centre, half-face shadow.
2. **MEDIUM** — current self, tired but still handsome, low-key chiaroscuro.
3. **TWO-SELVES, facing** — current self as a dark silhouette in the foreground; younger self lit across
   the field (Indonesia jersey), looking back. Matched distance.
4. **REVERSE** — younger self silhouette foreground; current self lit across (ZJU hoodie), looking back.
   SAME matched distance/scale as shot 3.
5. **CLOSE-UP** — younger self, dead-serious, off-centre, deep half-face shadow, sweat/grit.

Wardrobe: current = navy Zhejiang University hoodie; younger = red Indonesia national jersey.
Then animate each shot (Seedance 2.0, motion-only, identity locked) and cut together with real
transitions. Reuse this template for other "you vs your past/idol" pieces by swapping wardrobe + beats.
