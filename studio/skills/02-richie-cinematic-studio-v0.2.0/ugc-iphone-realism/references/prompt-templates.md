# Prompt templates — the copy-paste arsenal (Seedance 2.0 UGC)

Fill the brackets, run. Every prompt here is community-verified.

This file is the **canonical home** for the templates and examples — `iphone-look-and-realism.md`
links here rather than re-pasting them, so edit prompts in one place (here) and they never drift.

**Before you run any of these:** paste the Realism-Lock Stack at the END (see
`iphone-look-and-realism.md`). It carries the "visible pores / natural unevenness" skin spec
that defeats the waxy default — without it, viewers ID the render as AI inside one second.
Order rule still holds: keep **"Filmed with iPhone" first**, the negative last, realism-lock
appended after that. Keep total prompt 50–150 words; too long and conflicting instructions
break the natural feel.

## Master Template 1 — iPhone Look Stacking (universal · VIDEO AI ME)

The default. Stacks the six-part cluster inline; works for any scene. Brackets are the only
things you touch.
```
[Your scene description here].
Filmed with iPhone [front camera / rear camera], handheld [shaky energy / slight bounce],
natural [daylight / lamp light / window light / golden hour backlight],
[slight lens flare / colorful ambient reflections], casual framing.
- No music, no logo, no text on screen.
```

## Master Template 2 — UGC phone-in-hand, 5-part (WaveSpeed AI)

Best when one person talks to the lens while doing something. The labeled fields keep Seedance
from drifting — Subject/Action/Camera/Style/Constraints map to its prompt-weighting.
```
Subject: [person, age range, setting]
Action: [speaks casually about X while doing Y]
Camera: Medium, handheld phone perspective, slight sway, eye level, normal lens feel
Style: Natural indoor light, ungraded look, light motion blur
Constraints: No captions, no snap zooms, keep hands natural, 8-10s, keep background simple
```

## Master Template 3 — Full UGC ad brief (SeaImagine / Flyne)

For a complete ad with hook, demo, proof, and CTA. Use when you want one generation to carry
the whole spot. Remember: don't name the product before ~8s of content, or the viewer's
ad-detection kicks in and the rest runs against you.
```
Create a 9:16 UGC-style video for [product/service].
A [creator persona] films in [realistic setting] with casual handheld phone-camera energy.
Hook: "[first 2-second hook line]."
Show [product action/demo/tutorial] clearly.
Emphasize [main benefit] without exaggerated claims.
Add a believable proof moment: [texture, size, use case, comparison, routine, result].
Camera: close-up product shots, quick but readable cuts, natural motion, stable subject.
Mood: authentic, conversational, social-native.
Audio: [natural voiceover / ambient sound / soft music / product sound].
End with [CTA].
Duration: [8-15 seconds].
```

## Verified example prompts A–G

Each is a real, working prompt. Label = what it's best for. Swap brackets, append the

### A — Skatepark sneaker review · VIDEO AI ME
*Best for: high-energy outdoor product hold + try-on, golden-hour backlight.*
```
UGC creator, energetic Black man in his twenties standing in a concrete skatepark at golden
hour, holding a brand new pair of white and neon green sneakers. He lifts them close to the
camera lens, rotates them slowly saying: 'Bro look at these. Feel that material.' He drops
them on the ground, slides his foot in, stomps twice, then jogs three steps and stops. He
turns back to camera: 'Insane comfort.' Filmed with iPhone, warm sunset backlight, slight
lens flare, handheld. - No music, No logo, no text on screen.
```

### B — Indoor unboxing · Emma-mattress pattern
*Best for: reaction-driven unboxing; the handheld→tripod switch holds the iPhone look across shot types.*
```
UGC creator, a confused couple in pajamas standing in their small apartment. A massive
[product] box sits in the middle of the living room. The [person] rips it open aggressively,
[product] expands fast and they both jump back screaming. They throw it on the [surface], dive
onto it face first. The [person] rolls over, looks at camera and says: '[your line here].'
Filmed with iPhone, bedroom with warm lamp light, handheld for unboxing then locked tripod for
[next shot], chaotic energy. - No music, No logo, no text on screen.
```

### E — TikTok product review · r/AIAgentsInAction 550-video workflow
*Best for: the high-volume TikTok Shop spec; skin-texture line baked in, fully structured.*
```
UGC creator-style product review video. A [age range] [gender] in a [real everyday location:
kitchen / bedroom / gym / office], holding [product] naturally in one hand. Skin: realistic

watermark, no logo, no text overlay. Duration: 10-12 seconds.
```
