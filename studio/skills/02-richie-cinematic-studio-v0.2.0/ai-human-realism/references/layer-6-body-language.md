# Layer 6 — Body Language, Posture, Gesture (the behavioral layer)

Here realism shifts from rendering accuracy to **behavioral authenticity**. Studied as kinesics (body
movement), proxemics (space), haptics (touch). The scientific base is Paul Ekman's non-verbal
communication research.

## Gesture categories (Ekman) — useful for intent

| Category | What it is | Use |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Emblems | gestures with direct verbal meaning (thumbs up, OK) | intentional signals |
| Illustrators | gestures that reinforce speech | talking-avatar hand motion |
| Affect displays | body/face positions showing emotion | emotional expression |
| Regulators | behaviors controlling interaction flow | conversational turn-taking |
| Adaptors | self-touch, fidgeting | naturalism/realism markers |

## Posture communicates (macro signal)

- **Confidence vs. submission:** upright spine + open chest = confident; hunched shoulders + forward
  head = submissive/anxious.
- **Energy:** high energy uses more space; low energy contracts inward.
- **Engagement:** body oriented toward = engaged; away = disengaged.
- **Weight:** where weight sits signals comfort or tension.

Prompt vocabulary:
```
natural body language, upright relaxed posture, weight shifted naturally (slight contrapposto),
open chest and relaxed shoulders, body oriented toward [subject/camera], [or: closed guarded posture,
arms crossed, shoulders drawn in]
```

## Gesture authenticity — connect motion to intent

AI gesture fails three ways: repetitive cycling, emotionally flat-but-correct motion, and gestures
disconnected from the emotional content. **The fix is to describe the internal state driving the
gesture, not just the mechanics.** "He gestures with his hand" → flat. "He opens his palm as he makes
a point, leaning in slightly" → motivated.

## The "physical, not emotional" rule (most leverage)

Models execute bodies, not feelings. Translate every emotion into observable physical signals:
- ❌ "a sad atmosphere / he feels defeated"
- ✅ "his shoulders droop, his gaze falls to the floor, his hands hang loose at his sides"

## Idle motion — the breathing-realism layer (video)

A static human reads as artificial instantly. Add life:
```
subtle weight shifts (1–3° sway), natural breathing (chest and shoulders rise and fall),
involuntary blinks at irregular intervals (~15–20 per minute, not metronomic), small natural head drift,
fingers relaxing and tensing slightly
```
Irregularity is key — perfectly periodic blinks or sway read as robotic.
