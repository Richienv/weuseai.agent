# Layer 5 — Extremities: Hair, Eyes, Teeth, Hands (the "second-read" tells)

After the overall figure, the eye jumps to these. Failures here destroy realism even when everything
else is right.

## Eyes — the #1 believability signal (get these right first)

Eyes are the first and most critical uncanny-valley vector. Sub-elements:
- **Iris:** not a flat color disc — a radial fiber structure with depth. `iris texture, radial fibers,
  limbal ring, iris depth`.
- **Sclera:** never pure white — subtle yellow-pink with fine visible veins. `natural sclera, faint
  veins, not pure white`.
- **Cornea / catchlights:** specular reflections of the light source prove a light exists. A missing or
  misplaced catchlight is a strong tell. `catchlights in the eyes, specular highlight matching the key light`.
- **Eyelashes:** natural length variation and slight clumping; uniform lashes read fake.
- **Moisture:** eyes are always slightly wet — `subtle moisture on the lower lid, wet corneal sheen`.
- **Gaze:** both eyes must converge on exactly one target; even 1–2° misalignment is detected
  subconsciously. `aligned convergent gaze, both eyes fixed on the same point`.

## Hair — three separate problems

1. **Volume & structure:** how the mass sits and responds to gravity.
2. **Strand detail:** individual strands, especially baby hairs at the hairline catching light.
3. **Grooming authenticity:** real hair has flyaways and slight frizz unless explicitly styled.
```
individual hair strands visible, natural flyaways, baby hairs at the hairline, hair volume with gravity,
slight frizz, hair physics and movement
```

## Teeth

Off-white (not pure white), slightly yellowed at the base, more translucent at the edges; subtle size
variation; a wet sheen (not matte); natural gum-to-tooth transition.
```
slightly off-white teeth, natural enamel translucency at the edges, subtle size variation, wet sheen,
natural gum line
```
Avoid: pure-white uniform "veneer" teeth, matte teeth.

## Hands — the hardest extremity

Still the most failure-prone element. Prompt them explicitly and keep poses simple where possible.
```
anatomically correct hands, five fingers, natural relaxed finger position, correct knuckle detail,
natural nail detail, hands at rest [or: holding X firmly, not deforming]
```
Tactics: hide complexity (hands in pockets, behind back, partially out of frame), keep them relaxed,
and add to negatives: `no extra fingers, no fused fingers, no deformed hands, no warping objects`.
