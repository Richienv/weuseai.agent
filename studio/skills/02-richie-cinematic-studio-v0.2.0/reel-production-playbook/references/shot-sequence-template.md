# Shot sequence — "Now-You vs Younger-You" (Messi "YOU / NEXT LEVEL" style)

The proven structure. Two characters: the **current self** (built things abroad — navy Zhejiang
University hoodie) and the **younger self** (wanted to stay and build at home — red Indonesia
jersey). All shots on one premium standard (same camera/grade, only the lens changes). Story beat:
the man you became, facing the kid who had the other dream.

## The 6-shot cut (final, approved)

1. **S1 — WIDE.** Current self alone on a dark misty floodlit pitch, exhausted, off-centre,
   half-face shadow. Establishes mood + wardrobe.
2. **S2 — MEDIUM.** Current self, tired but composed and still handsome, low-key chiaroscuro.
3. **S3 — TWO SELVES (current's POV).** Current self foreground from behind (hood down, hair
   rim-lit by a hard backlight); younger self small and distant across the misty field, **face kept
   in shadow + mist**. Long shadow thrown forward.
4. **S4 — STANDOFF / reverse.** Younger silhouette foreground; current self lit across the field in
   the ZJU hoodie, lifting his head to look back.
5. **S5 — REVERSE POV (younger's POV).** Mirror of S3: younger self foreground from behind (red
   jersey, rim-lit); current self small and distant across the mist. Mirror the side.
6. **S6 — MEDIUM younger.** Younger self, dead-serious, half-face shadow, sweat/grit. The one
   clearly-lit face — a MEDIUM, never an extreme close-up.

## Rules that make it work

- **Hide the younger face** with distance + backlight + mist in the two-shots; only ever show it
  clearly in ONE medium (S6). Extreme close-ups expose the likeness gap.
- **Matched shot/reverse pairs.** S3 ↔ S5 are mirror POVs — keep the foreground-figure scale and
  the distant-figure scale matched, just swap the side and the wardrobe.
- **Same camera, only the lens changes.** Identical grade, grain, colour, mist across all six.
- **Naturalism = the ANGLE, not lower production value.** Off-centre, rule of thirds, slight
  handheld imperfection — but keep it premium and sharp.
- **Emotion:** current self = exhausted but composed (heavy eyelids, worn stare, faint sweat);
  younger self = dead-serious, intense.

## Reuse

Swap wardrobe + the two "selves" for any "you vs your past / your idol / your future" piece. Keep
the 6-beat spine: establish → medium → your POV across → standoff → their POV across → end on the
face that matters. Then animate each shot (see `seedance-motion-prompts.md`) and cut with hard cuts
(see `ffmpeg-stitch.md`).
