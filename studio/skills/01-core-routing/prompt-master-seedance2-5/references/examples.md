# Voice to clone

These are compressed English reconstructions of the user's gold prompts.
Clone the density and the contract tone. Do not clone the plot unless the
user is in that story.

When the user brainstorms, fill this same skeleton until it feels like these.

---

## 1 — Entrance, high corner, one door two faces

Use when a person enters a known room and a two-sided door must stay true.

```text
SCENE
Mid-1990s Hong Kong, late night. She comes home: the door is unlocked from
outside, cold green corridor light spills into the warm-red hall, she steps
in, turns, and shuts it. As the slab closes, the descending-tiger painting
on the outside face turns back to the corridor; the green wedge on the tiles
narrows and dies; the hall returns to a steady warm red. High-angle, one
continuous shot, no cut. Only she is in frame.

REFERENCES
<<<hall>>> — entrance master, interior 100%. Do not change: dark wood door
(this inner face is plain walnut), wall calendar, low cabinet with a floral
thermos and teacup, round mirror, red-shade lamp, red peony bead curtain on
the right, door mat, pair of beige slippers beside the mat, pale tiles. Every
object, material, and practical stays put. Do not add, delete, or move.
<<<image_1>>> — the same door's outside face: descending-tiger paint, old
metal handle and lock. This is not another door. Inner face = plain walnut
as <<<hall>>>. Outer face = tiger as <<<image_1>>>. The paint does not change
by a single stroke.
<<<girl>>> — the woman, 27, Hong Kong, slight, 158 cm, dark hair loosely
pinned, wine micro-stripe mandarin top, flared jeans, black Chelsea boots,
red brocade shoulder bag. Face, hair, clothes 100% as <<<girl>>>. Boots stay on.

PHYSICS
One door, two faces. Outside = tiger (<<<image_1>>>). Inside = plain walnut
(<<<hall>>>). Door swings into the room. During the open, from this interior
camera you can see the outside face angled on the open slab — the tiger
travels with the turn. When she shuts it, the tiger returns to the corridor
and the interior shows only walnut. Never two doors. Never paint the tiger
on the inside face. Never move the handle.

LIGHT
Night. The only source in the hall is the red-shade lamp on the low cabinet,
warm red, constant from first frame to last. It does not flicker. While the
door is open, cold green from the corridor lays a wedge on the tiles; cold
green and warm red meet on the floor. As the door closes, the green wedge
narrows and dies — occlusion, not a lamp change. Palette: warm amber #E99D25
and red #BA0101 inside; cold green #ADD794 only from the doorway; shadows
sink to #040011.

CAMERA
6 seconds. One continuous shot. Real time. Camera inside the hall, high
corner facing the door, about 2.3 m, about 40° down — a Wong Kar-wai high
angle: the door, the mat, the slippers, a sliver of the low cabinet, the
tiles. After she enters, crown and shoulders toward lens; her cheek is still
readable. Not a vertical top-shot. Camera does not move. Handheld breath
visible. 50mm anamorphic, wide open: focus nailed to the doorway and to her;
near edges heavy blur; vertical oval bokeh; halation on the lamp; 35mm
expired-film grain; wood, brocade, and worn tile read as real, never plastic.
Never push, pull, or zoom.

TIMELINE
0.0–1.0s: latch click; the door is pushed in from outside and swings into
the room — the green wedge lays onto the tiles; the open slab angles toward
lens and shows a corner of the outside tiger; beyond the doorway, a defocused
green night corridor.
1.0–3.0s: she comes in — slight body over the threshold onto the mat, boots
on; the red bag is a small red in the green.
3.0–4.5s: she turns, hand on the slab, pulls it shut — the tiger turns out
and leaves; walnut returns; the green wedge thins to a line and dies; latch
clicks.
4.5–6.0s: door sealed. Hall is only the lamp's warm red. She stands with her
back to the door for half a beat; a shoulder drops a fraction — she is home.

PERFORMANCE
Everyday, automatic coming-home: open, enter, shut without thinking. The
half-beat of easing after it is sealed is the only accent. Never: look at
lens, remove shoes, touch a light, extra glance-back, slow motion.

SOUND
Key and latch, a light hinge, two boot heels on tile, brocade bag tap, the
dull close plus latch, night-outside dying in the crack, then the quiet of
the hall. No dialogue.

LOCKS
1 One door, two faces: outside tiger as <<<image_1>>>, inside walnut as
<<<hall>>>; mid-open the outside face is visible from inside; after close
the tiger is gone. Never two doors. Never tiger on the inside face.
2 Hall 100% as <<<hall>>>: cabinet, thermos, cup, mirror, red lamp, calendar,
red beads, mat, slippers, tiles — same place, same material. Slippers are
still life; she never touches them.
3 Only source = the red lamp, constant. Green wedge = doorway occlusion.
No lamp on/off, no flicker.
4 High 40° at 2.3 m, fixed, no follow. One continuous shot, real time.
Handheld breath. No push, pull, zoom, or slow motion.
5 She as <<<girl>>>, boots on the whole take. No 1990s-and-later objects.
Never mirror-flip.
```

## 2 — Insert: two hands, one lock, two different tools

Use when the shot is a working insert. Frame is a vise. The rest of the
world is a smear of a known surface.

```text
SCENE
Mid-1990s Hong Kong, late night, tenement corridor. Extreme close-up: only
Li's two hands and the lock on her door. A thin steel pick in each hand,
practiced and hurried in the keyway. One continuous shot, no cut.

REFERENCES
<<<tools>>> — lock tools: long hand-forged steel needles and hooks, dark
silver, forge texture. One hooked, one straight. 100% as <<<tools>>>. Never
swap in a modern pick gun.
<<<hands>>> — Li's hands and cuff: olive-grey pinstripe sleeve, wine dotted
shirt, gold chain at the wrist, gold-buckle belt edge. Hands of a 35-year-old
tall man, knuckles readable. 100% as <<<hands>>>.
<<<image_1>>> — the outside of her door: dark panel, descending tiger, tall
old metal handle and lock. The lock lives on the metal body above or below
the handle. The close-up holds only the lock body and the hands; a scrap of
tiger sits in the blur. 100% as <<<image_1>>>.

LIGHT
Night. A warm-orange corridor globe rakes the hands and the lock. The rest
sinks. Cold green #ADD794 in the smear; warm amber #E99D25 on the hands;
shadow #040011. No lamp on/off.

CAMERA
5 seconds. Extreme close-up. One continuous shot, no cut. Real time. Camera
on the lock: only two hands and the lock body. Focus nailed to the keyway
and the tool tips; panel and background heavy blur. Handheld breath — a
person holding still to work, not a shake. 50mm anamorphic, wide open:
vertical oval bokeh, small vertical highlights on the steel, 35mm expired
grain, scratches in the steel, hair on the hands, brass wear on the lock.
Never plastic. Never push, pull, or zoom.

TIMELINE
0.0–2.0s: both tools already in the keyway; left hook braces, right needle
works, small testing twists.
2.0–4.0s: it does not give; he regrips, adds a little force, a little more
hurry; metal scrapes in the core.
4.0–5.0s: the hands load — the feel of almost open; two more presses. Hold
on the not-yet.

PERFORMANCE
Practiced thief hands: precise, muscle memory, hurried in the mind, not
sloppy. A little tension in the fingers. Never clumsy jabbing, never a
modern gun, never slow motion, never the wrong number of fingers.

SOUND
Fine scrape in the core, pins ticking, his held breath, corridor night air,
distant city. No dialogue.

LOCKS
1 Extreme CU: only two hands and the lock; a different <<<tools>>> tool in
each hand; focus on keyway and tips.
2 Door is the outside of her door as <<<image_1>>>; a scrap of tiger in the
blur; lock on the metal body.
3 Hands as <<<hands>>>.
4 5s, real time, one take, no cut; tiny breath only; no shake, slow motion,
push, pull, zoom; no lamp cue; no 1990s-and-later objects.
```

## 3 — Face hold: one recognition at a named second

Use when the take is a face and the plot already happened off-screen.
The whole shot performs one inner change. Mouth is dead.

```text
SCENE
One 5-second take. 300mm on her face. Camera in the bead doorway, looking
into the kitchen. She stands about two meters in, facing the doorway, face
left-of-center — head slightly down, chin in, eyes lifted under the brow to
a point beside the lens (the beads he just smashed through). A red rake
from the lamp and the beads cuts her crown, temple, and cheek; the other
side of the face stays cooler and darker. Telephoto crushes depth: soft
vertical bead-smears at the frame edges; kitchen lights melted. The man is
already gone — all of it off-screen, before frame 1. The take performs one
thing: the change in her eyes.

REFERENCES
<<<girl>>> — 29, slight East Asian woman, dark low bun, side-parted bangs,
wine stripe mandarin short sleeve. Appearance 100% as this still; same
person. Clean the whole take: no sweat, hair not wet, no face sheen, chin
and mouth corners dry, no droplets. Character appearance only.
<<<kitchen>>> — kitchen light master. In this take it is only the fully
melted backdrop: warm stove smear left, cold green window smear in depth,
one red lamp-dot upper right. Location light only, all defocused.

LIGHT
Red rake from upper right (lamp + bead bounce) on crown and cheek. Far side
held in cooler shadow. Never lift. Never flatten. Sources do not change;
only the beads' red bounce settles when the beads settle.

CAMERA
5 seconds. One continuous shot. Real time. Camera fixed in the bead doorway.
Handheld breath only. No push, pull, zoom, track, or rack. 300mm anamorphic
telephoto, wide open: only her face is sharp; pores readable; 35mm Kodak 500T
grain and halation; oval bokeh. 21:9. Off-center. Focus nailed to her face
for the whole take. Lips closed, soft, still.

TIMELINE
0.00–0.01: gaze locked on the beads, leftover hardness from the strike;
chest rises once from the effort; edge-bead smears whip once or twice, clatter,
then die back.
0.01–0.02: bead sound thins to stray clicks; breath moves to the nose; she
almost does not blink.
0.02: the recognition — eyes open a fraction (tiny, never a stare), brow
releases, the hardness leaves, a blank hold. Nothing else moves.
0.02–0.05: she holds the blank; breath slows; one almost invisible lash
tick; beads go still. Cut while the gaze is still on the same point.

PERFORMANCE
One job: leftover force → recognition → blank. Accent is 1 mm of eyelid at
0:02. Mouth never works. Never smile, cry, nose-wrinkle, bite, press, speak,
or look at lens. Face and chin stay dry.

SOUND
Loud diegetic 90s Hong Kong ballad from the boombox (in-scene, off to the
side). Under it: beads clatter once and decay to soft clicks, one heavy
breath settling to nose-breathing, faint night air. No dialogue, no lip
movement, no subtitles. No score except the diegetic song.

LOCKS
1 Only she is in frame; same person as <<<girl>>>; clean dry face the whole take.
2 Focus nailed to her face; beads stay foreground smear; kitchen stays
background smear as <<<kitchen>>>; no rack.
3 Recognition lands at ~0:02, 1 mm, then hold. Mouth closed and still.
4 5s, real time, one take, camera fixed in the bead doorway; breath only;
no push, pull, zoom, track.
5 Red rake from upper right, far side dark; never flatten; no lamp cue.
```

## 4 — POV: she is the camera, he is thrown out, door slams to black

Use when the audience is a person who must not appear, and architecture
plus scale plus the two-sided door all have to hold.

```text
SCENE
Mid-1990s Hong Kong, late night. POV — the camera is her eye. She stands
inside her own dark doorway. The door is open outward; the tiger (paint
exists only on the outside face) angles back toward the interior. At the
open, Li is still inside, two steps in front of her, in the dark-red hall,
hair wet-messy. A bouquet is thrown hard into his chest. Inertia walks him
backward over the threshold to the corridor rail. He blanks, then offense
climbs. The door is yanked shut — the tiger sweeps and leaves, the inner
walnut slab hits the jamb, the frame goes black. One continuous shot, no
cut. Only Li is in frame. She does not appear. The audience is her.

REFERENCES
<<<corridor>>> — what she sees through the open door, composition 100%:
dark-green iron rail across the frame, brown towel and two pale cloths on
it, a line of wooden pegs, wet cement with a green cast, dense night city
and vertical red neon beyond. He ends with his back on this rail, square
to lens. Do not change, do not mirror, do not change view.
<<<hall>>> — the interior world at the frame edges. Two facts: (1) brightness
— the hall is very dark, the red lamp is a low coal, everything else in the
well; (2) layout — red peony bamboo beads hang to the right of the door, so
the right edge of this frame is those beads, near, heavy blur, one or two
strands hanging in. Left and top edges are the dark jamb. Inner door face
is plain walnut as <<<hall>>>, no paint.
<<<image_1>>> — outside face of the same door: tiger, old metal handle.
Because the door is open out, that outside face now stands on the left edge,
half in the inside/outside mix. On the slam the tiger sweeps and leaves.
[LEE] — Li, 35, tall, 185 cm, wide 90s olive-grey pinstripe suit, wine
dotted shirt open at the throat, gold chain, gold buckle. Ears and sideburns
clean; only slightly wet wisps at the ear. Wet hair stuck to the forehead,
face sheen, dark wet on the shoulders.
<<<image_2>>> — the bouquet: pale pink peonies in a kraft cone, twine, one
stem clearly broken and hanging. After impact it is still this bouquet.

PHYSICS
Rail is the ruler: iron rail ~110 cm. Li is 185 cm — when he stands straight
on it, the top rail sits just above his belt, lower ribs, far below the
chest. Most of him is above the rail. The towel hangs to his thigh. His
shoulders are wider than the gap between two posts. Never a rail at chest
height, never a shorter man, never a rail that changes height.

Door opens out to the corridor. From this POV: hinge on the left of the
doorway; the open slab stands outside on the left, so the outside tiger
angles back at the interior lens — that is the left-edge smear at the open.
Slam = the slab is yanked around the left hinge: tiger sweeps left-to-right
and turns out; inner walnut as <<<hall>>> (no paint) hits the jamb, square
to lens — black. Never a door that opens in. Never tiger on the inside face.
Never tiger still visible after close. Never a hinge swap. Never a second door.

LIGHT
Expose for outside. Interior is much darker — looking from a dark room into
brightness. Three layers:
1 Darkest = interior: right-edge red beads and left-edge jamb in the well,
  only the low red #BA0101 of the lamp as <<<hall>>>. Interior is never bright,
  never glowing, never a field of red. Li at the open is in this well; you
  get silhouette and the water on his face.
2 Mid = slab and jamb, half and half.
3 Brightest = green night outside, #ADD794, city and neon melted. The instant
  he clears the threshold, the light on him switches from dark red to cold
  green — he moved through a zone, the lamps did not change.
No lamp on/off. End black = the slab, not a fade. Shadows to #040011.

CAMERA
7 seconds. One continuous POV shot, no cut, real time — no slow motion.
Camera = her eye at 148 cm, shorter than him, slightly up, he reads tall.
Camera is almost still — breath so small it is almost not there. No shake,
no sway, no handheld bounce. 50mm anamorphic, wide open: focus stays on Li
as he travels from interior red into outside green; city behind him melts.
Left edge = defocused tiger slab; right edge = defocused dark-red beads;
top = dark jamb — a dark frame around a brighter outside. Never push, pull,
or zoom.

TIMELINE
0.0–0.8s: Li in the dark-red hall, two steps from lens; the bouquet is thrown
from below frame, a fast flat arc, into his chest — kraft thump, two or three
petals drop.
0.8–2.0s: inertia walks him back — two or three catching steps, over the
threshold, out of the room; light on him goes from dark red to cold green;
his lower back finds the rail (just above the belt) and stops; he claws the
bouquet into his chest.
2.0–5.6s: his face is on lens, two beats given time: true blank — he looks
down at the flowers, looks up, he did not expect this (2.0–3.6); then offense
climbs into the eyes, a nostril flare, jaw set, he stares at lens, chest
working, no word yet (3.6–5.6).
5.6–6.6s: the door is yanked shut — tiger slab sweeps from the left and turns
out; inner walnut hits the jamb; a hard close plus latch; frame goes black.
No pause. No gentle close.
6.6–7.0s: black. Latch tail.

PERFORMANCE
A narcissist just refused and hit in the chest with flowers: blank → offense,
both beats played. Blank is real (the look down at the flowers). Heat climbs;
it does not switch. The retreat is inertia, legs finding floor, not a chosen
exit. Not sad. Not begging. Offended. Never: slow motion, speech, crying,
big head-whip, a slow door, walking himself out.

SOUND
Kraft thump, petals, his short air sound, two or three leather steps from
tile onto wet cement, waist on rail, corridor night (wind, far city, an AC
box), his nose-breath, then the slam plus latch, beads ticking once in the
door-wind, sound cut dead. No dialogue.

LOCKS
1 Open: Li in the interior well, two steps from lens. After the hit, inertia
takes him two or three steps over the threshold to the rail. He is thrown,
he does not walk out.
2 Scale: rail 110 cm, top just above the belt of a 185 cm man; most of him
above the rail; shoulders wider than a post gap.
3 Tiger only on the outside: angled to lens at the open as <<<image_1>>>;
gone after the slam. Inner face = walnut as <<<hall>>>, no paint.
4 Right edge = red bamboo beads, dark, hanging in; left edge = dark jamb.
Exposure: interior darkest, slab mid, outside brightest — interior never
bright. Light on him changes because he moved. Lamps do not.
5 Background = <<<corridor>>> : rail, towels, pegs, night red neon. He ends
on the rail, square to lens. Never a deep corridor view.
6 7s, real time, one POV take; camera almost still, tiny breath only; no
shake, sway, slow motion, cut, push, pull, zoom. No lamp cue. Black = slab.
Ears/sideburns clean. No 1990s-and-later objects.
```

## How to reuse these when the user brainstorms

1. Steal the section order and the sentence types, not the tiger door.
2. Decide the one job (enter and shut / work a lock / recognize / throw him out).
3. Decide who is in frame and who is the camera.
4. Write the object law before the acting.
5. Put the camera in a corner with a number.
6. Make light a lamp and an opening, not a mood.
7. Put the accent on a second.
8. Close with the five facts that would break first.

If a drafted prompt sounds like a trailer, it is wrong. If it sounds like a
1st AD telling the operator and the actor what must not happen, it is right.
